### What's in it

- **Five lithophane panels:** four sides and a top. Empty slots become plain panels that glow evenly.
- **A framed cube:** every face shows the same square window. A floor with a cable hole, and a lid that attaches with printed press-fit pegs or glued magnets.
- **Image controls:** crop, brightness, contrast, auto-contrast, gamma, sharpening, grayscale mode and invert.
- **Live 3D preview** with an exploded view, and **STL export** part by part or as one zip, already oriented for printing (panels stand upright).
- **A printing guide** at [/guide](https://lithophane-maker.merope.dev/guide/), and **test pieces** that check groove fit, lid fit and image brightness before you commit to a full print.
- **Warnings** for settings that would produce parts that can't be printed or won't fit together.

### Beta

The geometry is checked by automated tests. The physical fits are still being test-printed, so default clearances may change. If you print one, please [report how it went](https://github.com/ahmedsaed/lithophane-maker/issues/new).

Try it at [lithophane-maker.merope.dev](https://lithophane-maker.merope.dev).
