v0.2.1 loosened the fits after the first full cube was hard to assemble. The other four panels then slid in fine at the original clearance: the first panel's brim ridge was the problem, not the groove. The snapped peg came from leaning posts and putting the lid on at an angle, not from a tight hole.

### Changes

- **Groove clearance back to 0.20 mm and peg clearance back to 0.10 mm**, the values a full cube was actually assembled with. The frame is 14.4 mm wide again.
- **Deburr every panel edge.** The guide and the zip README have a new _Before you assemble_ step: peeling the brim leaves a ridge, and it is enough to make a well-fitting panel bind. The groove test now deburrs its strip too.
- **Press the lid straight down.** The tall posts can lean out a little until the panels are in; push them upright first, and take the lid on and off as little as possible.
- **Self-centring pegs.** Each peg tip tapers over 1.5 mm (was 0.6 mm), so it finds its hole when a post leans instead of catching on the rim.

### Mixing parts with earlier versions

v0.2.2 parts fit v0.2.0 parts: only the peg tip changed. Parts printed with v0.2.1 should be used together, since that frame is 0.05 mm wider.
