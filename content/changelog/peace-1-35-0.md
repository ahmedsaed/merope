### What's new

- Peace now opens in your theme: light for Paper and Mist, dark for Ledger and Graphite, instead of always dark. On Android 12 and later, a new theme shows from the next time you open the app.
- The Analysis chart is bigger. Tap or hold any part of it to see which category it is and its share of the month.
