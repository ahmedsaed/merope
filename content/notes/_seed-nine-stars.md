For a while the star field had six members. It looked fine. It was not the sky.

Atlas and Pleione form the cluster's eastern handle, and without them Alcyone
sits alone at the edge of the frame. The shape a person recognises as the
Pleiades simply is not there.

## Guessing is worse than a gap

One search result returned Asterope with Alcyone's right ascension — precise,
plausible, and wrong.
