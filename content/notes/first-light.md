Astronomers call a telescope's first real image **first light**. It is rarely a
good image. The point is that the instrument works end to end — mirror, mount,
camera, software — and that everything after it is refinement.

## Why a plate

Merope is the faint one. It is the sister you have to look for, and the star
whose only claim on the nebula around it is that it happens to be lighting
dust it did not make.

```ts
const thesis = 'The star is not the point. What it lights up is.';
```

That is a better description of a studio than anything with the word
_solutions_ in it.
