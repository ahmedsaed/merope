"Beta" means nothing. It meant something once, and then everyone used it to mean
"we shipped it and we are nervous", and now it is noise.

Stellar magnitude is inverted and logarithmic: lower is brighter, and six is the
limit of what an unaided eye can see. That boundary is not a convention someone
agreed to — it is a property of the eye.
